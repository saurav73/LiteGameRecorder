Meme sounds in this folder — where they came from
================================================

The 15 original sounds (whoosh, pop, ding, coin, boom, riser, airhorn,
applause, drumroll, click, laser, success, scratch, sad-trombone, punch)
were synthesized with numpy by generate_sounds.py — free to use, no licence.

The 10 meme sounds below were downloaded 2026-11-15 from sfxreels.com
(https://sfxreels.com/meme-sound-effects/). They are royalty-free
RE-CREATIONS made by sfxreels — not the original viral recordings — so
they are cleared for commercial use and will not trigger music detection
on Reels/TikTok. Converted here from MP3 to 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit WAV to
match the rest of the library.

- a-few-moments-later.wav — "A Few Moments Later" time-card sting
- bone-crack.wav — sharp crack for cringe gags
- bruh.wav — deadpan "Bruh" reaction (source file: bruh-speech.mp3)
- emotional-damage.wav — "Emotional Damage!" roast punchline
- fahh.wav — "Fahh" vocal stinger (the viral meme sound, as a legal re-creation)
- fbi-open-up.wav — "FBI Open Up!" (source file: fbi-open-up-speech.mp3)
- oh-no-laughing.wav — "Oh No No No" laugh (source file: oh-no-no-no-no-laughing.mp3)
- technologia.wav — "Technologia" music sting
- what.wav — confused "What?" reaction
- wow-anime.wav — anime "Wow!" (source file: wow-anime-meme-sound.mp3)

The real original "Fahh" / "Bruh" / "Emotional Damage" vocal clips are
copyrighted samples and cannot be bundled as the originals — these
re-creations are the legal way to get the same moments.

- dun-dun-dunnn.wav — "Dun Dun Dunnn" dramatic sting (three descending notes),
  synthesized with numpy on 2026-11-15 to complete the Auto-Edit mapping and
  the Dramatic Moment meme template pairing. Free to use, no licence.
