4. VOICE DELAY FIXED — this was the bug in your video: the mic
   started up to 1 second BEFORE the video (opening the webcam and
   starting game sound takes time), so your voice came 1 second
   AFTER your mouth moved. The app now measures the exact gap and
   moves the sound back onto your lips. Pausing is fixed too — the
   mic used to keep recording while the video was paused, so the
   sound drifted after every pause. Now paused parts are cut out
   of the sound. Tested: the voice lands exactly on the mouth now.

5. VOICE CHANGER WHILE RECORDING — you can now change the voice

6. EDITOR SOUND — REALLY FIXED THIS TIME — press Play in the
   editor and you hear the video sound + music + sound effects
   while you edit (before it was silent). There is a "Sound"
   tick box next to the Play button to turn it off.
   THE BUG (found by testing): the video used to start at once
   while the sound was still being prepared, so on a short clip
   the video FINISHED before the sound was ready — you heard
   nothing, and the status just said "Preparing sound…" forever.
   Now the video waits for the sound, and the mix is prepared
   in the background while you edit, so it is usually ready
   before you press Play.
   TWO PLAYERS: if the built-in player cannot play on your PC,
   the editor falls back to ffplay (it comes with the FFmpeg
   the installer downloads). If both fail, the editor TELLS you
   instead of staying silent.
   TEST IT IN 2 SECONDS: open the editor, go to the Audio tab,
   and press "Test sound (do you hear the Ding?)". If you hear
   the Ding, sound works on your PC — if you do not, the editor
   will say what is wrong.

HOW TO INSTALL THIS UPDATE
--------------------------
1. Close the app if it is open.
2. Double-click "Install Lite Game Recorder.bat".
3. Press "Install" and wait 2-3 minutes (it installs the libraries,
   builds the app, downloads FFmpeg for sound, and makes the
   Desktop icon). Your recordings are never deleted.
4. Double-click the Desktop icon to open it.

WHAT IS INSIDE
--------------
- app.py, editor.py, installer.py — the app + video editor + setup
- requirements.txt — the libraries (now includes packaging)
- LiteGameRecorder.spec, build.py, icon.png, icon.ico
- "Install Lite Game Recorder.bat" — one-click install
- "Uninstall Lite Game Recorder.bat" — removes the app, never recordings
- "Start Lite Game Recorder.vbs" — starts the app without a black window
- templates/ — 2 free example templates (Golden Hour Glow, Streamer Hype)
- sounds/ — 329 free sound effects for the editor (the 15 synthesized ones + Fahh, Bruh, Emotional Damage, Vine Boom, Roblox Oof, Windows XP Error, MLG Airhorn, Metal Pipe, and 300 more; see sounds/SOURCES.txt for where each came from)
- meme-videos/ — 100 meme video clips to drop into any video (time cards, reactions, fails, wins, dramatic, gaming)
- make_meme_videos.py — renders the meme video clips (python make_meme_videos.py rebuilds all 100)
- generate_sounds.py — makes the sounds again if you ever delete them (python generate_sounds.py)

GAME SOUND SETUP (one time, Windows)
-------------------------------------
If game sound still does not work after installing: right-click the
speaker icon -> Sound settings -> enable "Stereo Mix" (or install
the free VB-Cable). Then in the app press "Test game sound —
record 3 sec" and play something loud. It will tell you if it works.

HONEST LIMITS (still true)
--------------------------
- Smart Record's speech engine is NOT in the .exe — use run.bat
  for it. Character filters are a style look, not a real face-swap.
- No auto kill/win detection in a specific game, no cloud AI
  background removal, no 4K/60fps hardware encoding.
